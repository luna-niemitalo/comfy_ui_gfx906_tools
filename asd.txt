An imposing mature, matronly woman, deliberately abandoning civilization behind her to enter the wilderness. She is naked because she has deliberately discarded everything that was given to her, including her clothing. The scene represents rebellion, departure, refusal, and choosing the unknown desert over the shelter.

IMPORTANT SPATIAL RULE:

The image has two different spatial coordinate systems that must not be confused.

IMAGE SPACE:
Foreground/background refers to what is visually closer to or farther from the camera.

SUBJECT MOVEMENT SPACE:
Leading/trailing refers to the woman's direction of movement toward the desert.

Therefore:

Her leading foot is farther from the camera and is already in the desert.

Her trailing foot is closer to the camera and remains on the stone threshold.

These are different concepts.

The fact that the leading foot is farther from the camera does NOT make the trailing foot the leading foot.

The desert begins beyond the stone threshold and must remain visually separate from the stone beneath the trailing foot.
